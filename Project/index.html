About the Data

Agency: NYC 311 provides access to non-emergency City services. Managed by DoITT, 311 handles over 2M requests per year from residents reporting issues from noise to potholes.

Dataset: We use the 311 Service Requests from 2010 to Present. For performance, this site loads the most recent 1000 requests with location data.

Dev Reality Check 💻

Why it matters: 311 data is the city’s nervous system. Spikes in complaints predict quality-of-life issues before they hit the news. Our site lets you filter, map, and analyze requests so you can see patterns by neighborhood, time, and complaint type. Also so you can finally justify that Leaflet.js rabbit hole to your PM.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Information: Search and filter live 311 requests. Click any card for full details.
  • Analysis: Interactive charts showing when and where complaints happen. Switch views and zoom into time ranges.
  • Memes: Because if the data loads first try, did you even build it?

Requests Over Time

Insight: Requests tend to spike on weekdays and drop on weekends. Use the brush below the chart to zoom into specific weeks. Number go up = more problems = job security for civil engineers.
